🔒 Praman

A tamper-evident evidence engine for regulated AI in India

The Problem

"Prove this AI decision wasn't tampered with."

A bank approves a loan using an AI system. A customer later disputes the decision. The regulator asks a simple question: how do you know your own team didn't quietly edit the record after the fact? A database row isn't an answer. Whoever controls the database can edit it. Praman replaces "trust us" with a chain of events that makes editing detectable within milliseconds. Banking is just where the regulation arrived first — the same question applies anywhere an automated decision could be disputed: hiring, insurance claims, healthcare triage, content moderation.

What's real right now: 8 features working today, 3 stubbed and openly disclosed, 6 on the roadmap — see exactly which is which in the demo's maturity panel. Nothing here is presented as done that isn't.
